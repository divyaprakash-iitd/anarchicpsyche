It has been quite a while and I have had enough rest, I thought to myself as I turned on my side and tried to get a hold of my phone. It showed 4 o'clock in the evening. It indeed was time for me to end my siesta and get up. Lazily I put my foot down and proceeded towards the door leading to the bathroom to get fresh. I had a meeting to attend online and needed to make at least my face presentable.

I got to the door and held the doorknob but couldn't feel it. Things turned black and my eyes opened. I felt the warm bed underneath me. Turning around I saw the clock on the wall.

**4 PM**

What a strange dream, I thought to myself. But afternoon naps usually are responsible for such variants of dreams. My wife was sleeping next to me. I wondered if I should wake her up and tell her about it. I decided to let it go and get on with the routine to freshen up. I put my foot down and fell into an endless void.

I woke up with a sweaty forehead. The fall in the dream felt very real. Maybe I had made some noise in my dreams, as I could see my wife sitting next to me, trying to calm me down. I told her about the dream and she mocked me, thinking I was referring to the plot of the popular movie. I looked at the watch.

**4 PM**

In order to get some natural light to freshen up my senses, I pulled apart the curtains on the windows next to me. The sun was nearing the horizon, completing its last leg of the day's journey. In a moment the light from the sun went out and everything around me turned pitch black. After a moment's silence, the city erupted with roars of people from all around. My head started to hurt and I could feel darkness from the outside penetrating inside. I tried to shout but felt my consciousness fading away. The last thing I remembered was that I fell on my bed.

I got up but still couldn't see. I started panicking. It's all black now. I dare not open my eyes. I mean how long can this last? I'll probably be finally awake in a few hours. It's not like time runs very slowly in dreams. Right???
